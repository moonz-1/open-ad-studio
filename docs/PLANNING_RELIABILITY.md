# Source-bound creative planning

Local Qwen proposes three separately named objects (first, second, third). Each has ten strings: title, reason, tension, interpretation, support, backdrop, key, shadows, highlights and palette. All three must exist. The app never manufactures a missing direction or switches to Basic after an AI failure.

The application binds exact source quotations and fixed photographic IDs by slot. It frames the model's chosen support/backdrop around the unchanged original product, joins the model's lighting components, and supplies reference-supported camera, complete-product placement and copy space. Audience and emotional-response text are explicitly app-generated hypotheses based on the model's tension/title; the provider contribution records model fields and app fields separately. A proposed scene is never a verified generated image.

Owner guidance, approved palette preferences and labeled inferred style/audience hypotheses remain available. Hypotheses are not business evidence. Existing source, claim, count, packaging, geometry, lettering, identity and framing safeguards remain authoritative. Unsupported performance claims block new named proposals. The existing recorded claim-removal policy may remove an unsupported phrase from a complete model reason; replacing an unusable reason is rejected. Native text and every repair are retained.

Creative text uses SaveText's plain-text format. JSON serialization must not collapse duplicate keys before strict decoding. Known surrounding key whitespace may be normalized with provenance; duplicate/colliding keys, unknown fields and incomplete proposals fail. Prior full argument/concept records remain readable.

There are no automatic inference retries, new models or paid image jobs in this repair. The local execution limit remains 150 seconds and the generation ceiling remains 2,200 tokens. Model quality varies; source descriptions are text evidence, not pixel analysis. Directions require explicit review before an independently authorized image-generation request.

CPU regressions: named-planning, creative-contract, argument-planning, product-scenes and the existing full suite. Real acceptance evidence and native customer responses stay private under outputs/.
